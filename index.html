How fast can you respond to an emergency AC repair in Houston?

For emergency calls, we typically dispatch a technician within 2-4 hours. We offer 24/7 emergency service — nights, weekends, and holidays. Houston summers don't wait and neither do we.

What brands of AC units do you service?

We service all major brands including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, York, Goodman, American Standard, and more. Our technicians are certified to work on virtually any residential or commercial HVAC system.

How much does AC repair cost in Houston?

Most residential AC repairs range from $150 to $600 depending on the issue. Common repairs like capacitor replacement run $150-250. Refrigerant recharge is $200-400. We always provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

When should I replace my AC instead of repairing it?

If your AC is over 10-12 years old and repair costs exceed 50% of a new unit's price, replacement is usually the better investment. We'll give you an honest recommendation — not one that benefits us.
