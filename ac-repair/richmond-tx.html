Reading the new efficiency numbers before you replace

If your Richmond system is ten or more years old, the efficiency label on a new one will not look like the old one. Since January 1, 2023, air conditioners have been rated in SEER2, a tougher test that produces lower numbers for the same equipment. Texas falls in the Department of Energy's Southeast region, where a new split-system air conditioner must reach at least 14.3 SEER2 below 45,000 Btu, roughly the old 15 SEER, and 13.8 SEER2 at larger sizes. Heat pumps nationwide must reach 14.3 SEER2 and 7.5 HSPF2.

Label you might seeWhat it means
13 or 14 SEER on your old unitThe old test. Below today's Southeast minimum.
14.3 SEER2The legal minimum for most new split systems here.
16 to 18 SEER2Usually two-stage or variable-speed equipment. Lower bills and better humidity control, at a higher price.

A minimum unit cools the house. A higher-rated two-stage or variable-speed unit runs longer at low speed, which pulls more moisture out of the Brazos River air and evens out the temperature between rooms. Whether it pays back depends on how long you plan to stay.

Size it to the house, not the old unit

Richmond has two kinds of houses. Pecan Grove and the older neighborhoods have systems that have been replaced once or twice, sometimes by copying whatever size was there. Aliana, Harvest Green and the newer communities have large, open two-story plans with tall ceilings and lots of west-facing glass. In both, the right size comes from a load calculation, commonly called a Manual J, that accounts for the square footage, windows, insulation and orientation. An oversized system cools in short bursts and leaves the house clammy; an undersized one runs all afternoon and never catches up. Ask for the calculation before you accept a quote.

Heat pump or gas furnace

Many Fort Bend houses pair the air conditioner with a gas furnace. When it is time to replace both, a heat pump is worth pricing: it heats with the same equipment that cools, and Richmond winters are mild enough that it will rarely need its backup heat. A good technician will compare both honestly for your house and gas rates rather than steering you toward one.

Common questions

What SEER2 rating does a new AC need in Richmond, TX?

Texas is in the DOE Southeast region, where new split-system air conditioners below 45,000 Btu must reach at least 14.3 SEER2, and larger ones 13.8 SEER2. Heat pumps must reach 14.3 SEER2 and 7.5 HSPF2.

Is SEER2 the same as SEER?

No. SEER2 uses a tougher test, so the number is lower for the same equipment. 14.3 SEER2 is roughly equal to the old 15 SEER.

How should a new system be sized?

With a load calculation such as a Manual J that accounts for the house itself, not by copying the size of the old unit.

Should I switch from a gas furnace to a heat pump?

It is worth pricing when both need replacing. Richmond's mild winters suit heat pumps, but the answer depends on your house and gas costs.
