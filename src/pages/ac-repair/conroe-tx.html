Entergy Texas or CenterPoint: who delivers your power

Much of greater Houston has one wires company and a long list of retail providers. Conroe is different. Look at your bill to see which situation you are in.

If your bill says Entergy Texas

Entergy Texas is a regulated utility. Its Southeast Texas service area was never opened to retail competition, so you buy electricity from Entergy itself and report outages to Entergy. Entergy has done reliability upgrades in Conroe and restored service here after storms.

If your bill names a retail provider

You are likely in CenterPoint's delivery area. CenterPoint keeps up the poles and lines and fixes outages; the bill itself comes from whichever retailer you signed with. You can switch retail providers; you cannot switch wires companies.

Storm outages and what to do when power returns

Severe storms reach Conroe as well as the coast. After storms in late May 2025, Entergy Texas reported a peak of 31,760 customers without power across its Southeast Texas system early on May 27, and by the next afternoon most of the remaining outages were in the Conroe area. When the power comes back after a long outage, a few steps protect your system:

  1. Wait a few minutes before asking the AC to run, so the power has a chance to settle.
  2. Check the breaker for the outdoor unit and give a tripped one a single reset, and if it flips off a second time, stop.
  3. Look outside for branches or debris against the condenser and pull them clear with the system off.
  4. Call if it will not cool after an hour or two of running, or if you hear grinding, buzzing or clicking without the fan starting.

Lake Conroe homes that sit empty

Plenty of houses around the lake are weekend places or second homes. In a Gulf Coast summer, shutting the air conditioner off while you are away lets the humidity inside climb, because the system is also the main thing drying the air. Before you leave for a stretch, set the thermostat higher rather than off, replace a dirty filter, and look at the condensate line where it drains outside to make sure it is not plugged. A thermostat that alerts your phone when the indoor temperature climbs can warn you before a breakdown turns into a moldy closet.

Entergy Texas efficiency programs

If Entergy delivers your power, ask about its energy efficiency offers before you pay for maintenance or upgrades. Entergy Texas has held CoolSaver events in some of the communities it serves, where its efficiency team worked with local contractors to give qualifying customers A/C tune-ups at no cost, and some homes also received weatherization such as air sealing, attic insulation and duct sealing. Locations, programs and eligibility change, so confirm the current terms with Entergy and ask the HVAC company whether it takes part.

Before you approve work in Conroe

  • Ask about permits. Conroe has its own city government. Find out from the contractor if the job calls for a City of Conroe permit and inspection, and have it written on the quote. If your Conroe address is outside city limits, ask what applies there instead.
  • Ask about the warranty. For a newer home, ask whether the part is covered and what labor will cost.
