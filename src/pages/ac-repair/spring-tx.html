Spring: a railroad town that became a suburb

Spring grew up around a railroad that opened through the area in 1871. By 1910 it had about 1,200 people. After the railroad moved its roundhouse to Houston in 1923, the town shrank to around 300 people by 1931. The original town, now Old Town Spring near Spring-Cypress and Hardy roads, was restored as a shopping district starting in 1979.

Today the Spring census designated place counted 62,559 people in 2020, and the Spring name stretches across a large part of northern Harris County and a small piece of Montgomery County. Because of that sprawl, a Spring address can mean a small older cottage near the historic district, a 1980s ranch home or a brand new two-story in a master planned community, each with very different equipment.

Unincorporated Spring: who handles what

Without a city, the jobs a city hall would do are split up. This is how it sorts out for a heating and cooling problem.

The HVAC company

Diagnoses, prices and repairs or replaces your system. Texas requires it to hold an Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Contractor license from TDLR whether or not you live in a city.

Your electric bill

Names the company that delivers your power and restores outages. If the house is dark, report it there. If the lights work and the AC does not, call an HVAC company.

Your HOA

If your subdivision has deed restrictions and a replacement moves the outdoor unit or adds equipment, check whether the association wants to approve it first.

When it quits at 2 a.m.

A lot of people searching for 24 hour AC repair in Spring are sitting in a warm house in the middle of the night. A few safe checks can save a call or help the technician later:

  1. Thermostat: confirm it is on Cool, set below the room temperature, and has fresh batteries if it uses them.
  2. Filter: a filter caked with dust can choke airflow until the indoor coil ices over. Replace it if it is dirty.
  3. Breaker: reset a tripped AC breaker once. If it trips again, leave it off and call.
  4. Water: look for water around the indoor unit. Some systems shut themselves off when the drain backs up.

Anything beyond those checks, including refrigerant, wiring and parts inside the cabinets, belongs to a technician. Large storms such as Hurricane Beryl in July 2024, which left hundreds of thousands of Houston-area customers still without power five days later, are a reminder to know where your breaker panel is and how your thermostat works before you need to.

Heating repair in Spring when the weather turns

The first cold mornings of fall bring calls about furnaces that will not light and heat pumps blowing cool air. Check the thermostat is on Heat, the filter is clean and the breaker has not tripped. If you have a gas furnace and smell gas, leave the house, then call the gas company named on your bill or 911 from outside. If a carbon monoxide alarm goes off, get everyone out and call 911.

Services Spring homes ask about most

Start with AC repair for warm air, ice or a unit that will not start. If it is the middle of the night or the house is getting dangerously hot, read emergency AC repair. For an older system that keeps failing, repair or replace walks through the decision, and heating repair covers furnaces and heat pumps.
