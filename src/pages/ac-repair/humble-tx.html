Humble: an oil boom town that the airport woke up

The town takes its name from Pleasant Humble, who ran a ferry on the San Jacinto River and served as justice of the peace in the late 1800s. Oil changed everything. After small finds on Moonshine Hill in 1904, a well that came in on January 7, 1905, flowed about 8,500 barrels a day, and the Humble field became the largest producer in Texas that year. A village of a few hundred people swelled to a boom town. In 1911 a group of local operators formed an oil company named for the field, one of the companies that later became part of Exxon.

Humble incorporated in 1933 and stayed a quiet small city until Houston Intercontinental Airport, now George Bush Intercontinental, opened next door in 1969. Growth has rolled outward ever since, which is why so many of the homes people call about sit in unincorporated subdivisions rather than in the old town.

City of Humble or not: why your address matters

Before anyone talks about permits, figure out which side of the line your house is on. The city is compact. Atascocita alone counted about 88,174 residents in the 2020 census, more than five times the city's population, and it is not part of Humble at all. Plenty of homes with a Humble mailing address are in unincorporated Harris County too.

Inside Humble city limits

The City of Humble runs its own Building and Inspection department at 114 West Higgins Street. Its published rules say that work done before a required permit is issued is charged a fee equal to double the permits. Homes in a floodplain may also need a floodplain development permit, even for work that does not need a construction permit. Ask the HVAC company whether your replacement needs a city permit and have it listed on the quote.

Atascocita and other unincorporated areas

There is no city permit office to deal with. That does not lower the bar for the contractor: Texas still requires an Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Contractor license from TDLR. Ask the company what, if any, permits apply at your address, and look up its license yourself before work starts.

What Harvey taught Humble about outdoor units

Humble, Kingwood and Atascocita sit between Lake Houston, the West Fork of the San Jacinto River, Spring Creek and several bayous. In August 2017, Hurricane Harvey dropped more than 30 inches of rain on parts of the Lake Houston area in four days.

  1. The damage was close to home. Humble's mayor at the time put the city's toll at about 370 homes and more than 40 businesses flooded, and the wider Lake Houston area was hit far harder.
  2. Upstream changes followed. Since 2018, Lake Conroe has been lowered on a seasonal schedule, one foot in April and May and two feet in August and September, to reduce flood risk downstream. Humble's council backed continuing it in 2023.
  3. A flooded unit is not a restart. If water reaches your condenser or an indoor unit in a garage, leave it off at the thermostat and the breaker until a technician has checked it.
  4. Plan the next install higher. When a system in a low-lying spot is due for replacement, ask about mounting the new condenser on a raised stand or platform as part of the job.

Airport heat: the numbers right next door

Humble's nearest official weather station is at Bush Intercontinental. The all-time high recorded there is 109 degrees, first set in 2011 and tied on August 24, 2023. When a summer runs that hot, an air conditioner sized and charged correctly can still run nearly nonstop through the afternoon, and any weak part shows up fast. A tired start capacitor, a clogged condensate drain or a dirty outdoor coil that coped in June can fail on a 105 degree day in August.

That is also why a system that cools fine in the morning but falls behind by 4 p.m. is worth a call, not just a lower thermostat setting. Turning the thermostat way down does not make the air come out colder. It just keeps the system running longer.

Simple checks before you call for AC service in Humble, TX

  • Thermostat on Cool and set below room temperature, with fresh batteries if it has them.
  • A clean filter. Pine pollen in spring can load a filter faster than you expect.
  • The breaker. One reset of a tripped AC breaker is fine. A repeat trip is your cue to stop.
  • Room to breathe outside. Rake pine straw and leaves away from the condenser and keep shrubs trimmed back.
  • Water at the indoor unit. A backed-up drain can trip a safety switch that shuts cooling off.

Leave refrigerant, wiring inside the unit and anything involving gas to a technician. If you smell gas, get everyone out, then call 911 and CenterPoint Energy's gas leak line (713-659-2111 or 888-876-5786) from a safe spot. If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, leave the house and call 911.

Power and gas in the Humble area

Humble homes are in CenterPoint Energy's electric delivery area and in the competitive market, so CenterPoint owns the wires and handles outages while a retail provider of your choosing bills you. The Public Utility Commission of Texas runs Power to Choose for comparing those plans. CenterPoint is also the natural gas company for much of the Houston area; check your gas bill to confirm who serves your address. None of these companies repairs air conditioners or furnaces. Power on but a silent condenser is an HVAC problem.
