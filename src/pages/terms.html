What the line does

The line is a free referral phone service. An automated assistant answers, asks about your heating or cooling problem and transfers your call to an independent, locally owned HVAC company that serves the Houston area. If that company cannot answer, your details are passed to it so it can call you back. The line is not an HVAC contractor. It does not inspect, diagnose, repair, install, service, quote, schedule or supervise any work, and it holds no HVAC license.

Your agreement is with the company you hire

Each company is a separate business. It decides whether to take your job, inspects the system, sets its own prices, does the work and bills you. Payment, warranties, cancellation and any dispute about the work are between you and that company. Ask for its price, scope and warranty in writing before work starts. The line is not a party to that agreement and makes no promise about any company's availability, timing, price, workmanship, licensing or insurance.

Fees

Calling the line and being transferred costs you nothing. Companies may pay the line a referral fee. That fee is not added to your bill by the line; ask the company how it prices its work.

Check the license yourself

The Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) licenses air conditioning and refrigeration contractors. Ask any company for its license number and look it up on the TDLR license search (opens in a new tab) before you hire it. The line does not verify licenses for you.

The guides on this site are general information, researched from the public sources listed on each page. They are not a diagnosis of your system, a quote or professional advice for your property, and rules, programs and prices change. Cost figures on the site are published third-party ranges, not prices from the line or from any company.

Safety comes first

If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility's emergency number or 911 from outside. If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds or someone shows signs of heat illness, call 911. Do not wait for an HVAC company in an emergency.
