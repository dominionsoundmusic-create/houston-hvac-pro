When the power comes back, protect the compressor

Humble and Atascocita learned in July 2024 what a long outage does to air conditioners. When Hurricane Beryl knocked out power to more than two million CenterPoint customers across the Houston area, many homes near Lake Houston were without it for days. When the lights came back, a lot of systems did not.

The reason is usually electrical, not the storm itself. Power often returns unevenly, with dips and surges as circuits are switched back on. A compressor that tries to start into low voltage strains hard, and the capacitor that helps it start is the first thing to fail. Sometimes it is the compressor.

  • During an outage, turn the thermostat to off.
  • When power returns, wait until it has been steady for a while before turning cooling back on.
  • If the outdoor unit hums but the fan does not spin, or it clicks and stops, turn it off and call. Running it that way can cook the compressor.

Hard-start kits and surge protection

Two inexpensive add-ons are worth asking about in the Humble area. A hard-start kit gives an older compressor extra help getting going, which reduces strain on every start, not just after an outage. A surge protector at the outdoor disconnect helps shield the electronics in newer systems, which are more sensitive than the old ones. Neither is magic, and neither replaces a sound system, but on a house that loses power every storm season they are cheap insurance.

Humidity by the lake

The neighborhoods along Lake Houston and the San Jacinto sit in some of the dampest air around Houston. An air conditioner removes humidity as well as heat, and when it cannot keep up the house feels clammy even at 74 degrees. Common causes are an oversized system that cools too fast and shuts off before it has dried the air, a dirty coil, or low airflow. Drain lines also clog fast here, so a float switch that shuts the system off before the pan overflows is worth having.

Common questions

Why didn't my AC work after the power came back?

Uneven voltage and surges during restoration often take out the start capacitor, and sometimes strain the compressor. Keep the thermostat off during an outage and wait for steady power before restarting.

What is a hard-start kit?

A small add-on that gives the compressor extra starting torque. It reduces strain on older compressors, especially in areas with frequent outages.

Why does my house feel damp even when it is cool?

The system is not removing enough moisture, often because it is oversized, the coil is dirty or airflow is low. It is common near Lake Houston.

Is Atascocita in the City of Humble?

No. It has a Humble address but is unincorporated Harris County. The contractor's Texas license applies either way.
